My Story • 5 Years of Rhythm

Partnering, Percussive Footwork & Playful Curiosity

My love affair with dance began in a Ballroom class at fifteen—and when I returned to the studio in 2021, I dove in headfirst and never looked back. Over the past five years while pursuing my Bachelor of Arts in Dance at Brigham Young University, I’ve built a movement vocabulary rooted in joy, musicality, and fearless experimentation.

For four years, I performed with BYU’s collegiate folk dance teams—appearing every semester in Christmas Around the World and Festival of Nations—immersing myself in cultural styles from across the globe alongside heavy training in clogging and tap.

As Tap Club President and a choreographer who isn’t afraid to strap on tap shoes for an aerial silk routine, I love finding the unexpected places where styles meet—and sharing that spark with my students every week.
